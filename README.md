# EthnoHarvest AI

Team Eco Roots (Shruthi and Vishnu), EthnoHack 2026, Track 3.

Full stack demo: FastAPI backend and React frontend served from one container.

**Demo only.** The HarvestOps demo workspace holds synthetic, watermarked data. Do not upload real documents.
A Harvest Passport is not a permit. Data written here is reset whenever the Space restarts.

## Data and licences

* COMPADRE Plant Matrix Database v6.26.3.0, CC BY SA 4.0. Only derived files (`data/clean/compadre_*`) are included,
  and they are shared under the same licence. Please get the database itself from compadre-db.org.
* World Checklist of Vascular Plants, Royal Botanic Gardens Kew, CC BY 3.0 (Govaerts et al. 2021, Sci Data 8:215).
* World Flora Online, open data. TDWG WGSRPD level 3 boundaries.
* USDA Dr Duke databases (CC0), Herbolario via GBIF (CC BY 4.0), PhenObs (CC BY 4.0), GBIF (CC BY 4.0 / CC0 per record).
* WorldClim 2.1 is downloaded from its official host at build time and is not redistributed here (Fick and Hijmans 2017).
* Photos carry their own open licence and credit in the app.
